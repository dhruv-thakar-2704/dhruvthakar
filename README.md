# 💫 About Me

# Hi, I'm **Dhruv Thakar** 👋

I am **Dhruv Thakar**, an MBA candidate specializing in **Logistics & Supply Chain Management** at **Parul University**, with a **B.Tech in Petrochemical Engineering** from Pandit Deendayal Energy University (PDEU). I am passionate about solving real-world business and supply chain challenges through **operations management, process optimization, analytics, and technology**.

My experience spans **logistics operations, warehouse management, procurement, transportation coordination, dispatch planning, ERP-based operations, manufacturing, and supply chain process improvement**. I have a strong interest in **Supply Chain Consulting, Logistics Solution Design, Operations Excellence, and Data-Driven Decision Making**. I enjoy analyzing operational workflows, identifying bottlenecks, developing process improvements, and using analytical tools to convert business data into actionable insights.

My technical and analytical toolkit includes **Advanced Excel, Business Analytics, Python, ERP systems, Microsoft Office, SCADA awareness, and AI tools**. I have also worked on projects involving **warehouse throughput, transportation utilization, dispatch workflows, and interactive Excel dashboards**.

Beyond industry experience, I am actively involved in **research, conferences, and academic projects**, including international research publication and conference presentations. I was awarded the **2nd Rank for Best Paper Presentation** at ICSUMMIT 2026 and received the **Nanoscale Advances Prize Award from the Royal Society of Chemistry**.

This GitHub profile showcases my **projects, analytics work, research, technical learning, and supply chain solutions**, reflecting my goal of building practical, data-driven, and innovative solutions for modern business and logistics.
